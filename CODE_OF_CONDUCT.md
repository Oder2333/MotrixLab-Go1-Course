# Code of Conduct

MotrixLab follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We expect participants to be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Harassment,
personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, and unsafe instructions are not
acceptable in issues, discussions, reviews, commits, or other project spaces.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `developers@motphys.com`. The
maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation
when necessary to protect the community.
